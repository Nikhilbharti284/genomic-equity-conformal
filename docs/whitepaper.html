Genomic Equity Conformal (v3.0.0): Air-Gapped Clinical Calibration, Ancestry Equity & Regulatory QA Sidecar

Author: Nikhil Bharti (ORCID 0009-0006-3130-9141, GitHub @Nikhilbharti284) • Release: v3.0.0 (November 2026) • License: MIT (Code) + CC BY 4.0 (Data)

1. Abstract

Clinical variant pathogenicity predictors exhibit severe probability miscalibration and elevated Variant of Uncertain Significance (VUS) rates for underrepresented South Asian (SAS) and African (AFR) cohorts. Genomic Equity Conformal v3.0.0 is a zero-GPU, air-gapped clinical QA sidecar combining homology-aware splitting (100 clusters, 118 genes, 9,142 ≥2-star ClinVar GRCh38 variants), nested out-of-fold Platt scaling, non-empty Mondrian conformal prediction, and a 3-Layer BA1 Safeguard protecting balanced-selection alleles such as HBB_E7V (Sickle Cell).
