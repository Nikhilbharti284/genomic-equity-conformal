Section 1 • Executive Overview

Why Genomic Equity Matters & Blueprint Selection

Fixing EUR-biased calibration, SAS/AFR VUS burden, HBB_E7V sickle-cell false-benign traps, and DPDP Act 2023/2025 hospital data sovereignty.

Clinical & Regulatory Problem

Clinical VEPs trained on European cohorts miscalibrate South Asian (SAS) and African (AFR) patients and falsely call balancing-selection alleles like Sickle Cell (HBB_E7V) benign. Cloud SaaS suites also conflict with India's DPDP Act 2023/2025. Genomic Equity Conformal runs 100% air-gapped on free CPU.
